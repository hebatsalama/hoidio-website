Common questions

What does Hoidio cost?

Everything for your own medicines is free, and so is inviting caregivers. Following someone else as a caregiver needs Hoidio Family: €4.99 a month or €49.99 a year. The first 14 days are free. One caregiver buys it, and it covers up to two people and three caregivers in total.

Do I need an account?

No. Without an account, everything stays on your phone. An account lets you back up your information and share it with caregivers.

How do I sign in?

Go to More → Your account. Continue with your Apple or Google account, or type your email address and we email you a 6-digit code to type in the app. There is no Hoidio password.

The code didn't arrive

Wait a minute and check your spam or junk folder. The email comes from Hoidio <noreply@hoidio.com>. Each code works once and for a limited time, so if it has expired, ask for a new one.

I don't get reminders

Check that notifications are allowed for Hoidio in your phone's Settings, and that reminders are turned on in More → Reminders & settings. Reminders work in the app on your phone, not in a web browser. Signing out stops them until you sign in again.

Why don't my reminders show the medicine's name?

So that nobody else can read it on your locked phone. Open Hoidio to see which medicines are due. To show the names, turn on Show medicine names in reminders in More → Reminders & settings.

How do I add a caregiver?

Go to More → Share with a caregiver and create a code. The caregiver types it in their own Hoidio app under “People I care for”. The code works once, for 48 hours. You choose whether they see everything or only your medicine schedule. Inviting is free for you. The caregiver needs Hoidio Family, and the first 14 days are free.

How do I remove a caregiver?

Open More → Share with a caregiver and remove the person. They lose access immediately.

How do I cancel or restore Hoidio Family?

Cancel in your Apple or Google account settings, or open them from More → People I care for → Manage subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for. After that, every caregiver on the plan stops seeing the people they follow, and alerts stop. On a new phone, choose Restore purchases on the subscription screen.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app, go to More → Your account and tap Delete my account. Your account, the information saved in it and your caregiver connections are deleted straight away. This cannot be undone. If you have a Hoidio Family subscription, cancel it separately in your Apple or Google account, because deleting your account does not cancel it.

The information on your phone stays, so you can keep using Hoidio without an account. To remove it too, use Delete all my data in More → Reminders & settings, or uninstall the app.

If you can't open the app, email support@hoidio.com from your account's email address and we will delete your account within 30 days.

Is my information safe?

Your information is stored in the EU, and in the app only you and the caregivers you invite can see it. A few service providers help us run Hoidio, and we never sell your information or show ads. Read the full privacy policy.
