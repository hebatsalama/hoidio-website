Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Tietosuojaseloste suomeksi

This policy explains what information the Hoidio app handles, why, where it is stored and what rights you have. We have written it in plain language. If anything is unclear, email support@hoidio.com.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Laryngology Courses Oy (Business ID 3421195-7), Munkkiluodonkuja 6 A 2, 02160 Espoo, Finland. Contact for all privacy matters: support@hoidio.com.

2. The short version

  • You can use Hoidio without an account. Then everything you enter stays only on your phone.
  • If you sign in, your information is also saved in our database in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany) so it is backed up and can be shared with caregivers you choose.
  • In the app, only you and the caregivers you invite can see your information. A few service providers handle some of it so that Hoidio can work (section 5).
  • We ask for your explicit consent before handling your health information, and you can withdraw it at any time.
  • There are no ads, no tracking or analytics, and we never sell your information.

3. What information we handle

Information you enter in the app

Medicines (name, strength, dose, times, instructions, purpose, prescriber, storage place, expiry date and batch number), doses marked as taken, refill dates and prescription details, doctor visits, side effects, symptoms and allergies, measurements (blood pressure, pulse, blood sugar, weight, pain score), water intake, daily “I'm OK” check-ins, emergency contacts (name, relationship, phone number) and your app settings.

Much of this is health information, which the law treats as a special category of personal data. We handle it only to provide the app to you.

Account information (only if you sign in)

Your email address, the name you choose to show, your phone's time zone and language, and a technical notification address (push token) so that reminders and alerts can reach your phone. If you continue with Apple or Google, that service tells us your email address and a sign-in ID; we do not receive your password.

Consent records

When you give or withdraw a consent in the app, we save which consent it was, its version and the date, so that we can show that you gave it. Before you sign in, this record stays on your phone.

Hoidio Family subscription (only if you buy one)

Whether your subscription is active, which product you bought, its dates and which store it came from, linked to a random user ID. Apple or Google handles the payment; we never see your card details.

Caregiver information

When you invite a caregiver, we store the link between your accounts, the access level you chose (everything, or the medicine schedule only), the order in which caregivers are alerted, and a short log of alerts already sent so they are not repeated. Invite codes expire after 48 hours. We also tell you if a caregiver's subscription has ended, so that you know they no longer get your alerts.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts list, photos or browsing activity.

Withdrawing your consent for health information deletes that information; your account can stay. Withdrawing does not affect what was done before.

Apple and Google also act as independent controllers under their own privacy policies when you buy a subscription in their store, or when you choose to sign in with your Apple or Google account.

A caregiver notification contains only short alert text: your name and a time, such as “Heba's 8:00 reminder has not been confirmed”, “Heba's daily check-in has not been answered” or “Heba's weekly summary is ready”. It never includes medicine names, doses or other health details; the caregiver sees those only inside the app. This text passes through Expo and Apple or Google on its way to the caregiver's phone. When information is transferred outside the EU, we rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, and otherwise on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your reminders on your own phone are created by the phone itself and do not pass through our servers. By default they don't show medicine names; you can turn names on in More → Reminders & settings.

6. How long we keep it

  • Without an account: on your phone until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.
  • With an account: as long as you have the account. Signing out removes the information from your phone, including saved copies of people you follow; it stays in your account until you sign in again. You can delete your account at any time in the app (More → Your account → Delete my account); your information is then removed from our database straight away. If you ask us by email instead, we delete it within 30 days. Backups held by our database provider are cleared on their normal cycle.
  • Subscription: for as long as the subscription lasts. Purchase records stay with Apple, Google and RevenueCat, and we keep bookkeeping records for as long as Finnish accounting law requires.
  • Consent records: as long as you have the account.
  • After a Hoidio Family subscription ends: the caregivers it covered can no longer see your information or get alerts about you, and we tell you in the app. We keep the link between you and those caregivers for up to 12 months so that following can restart easily, then remove it. Your own information stays in your account as described above. You can remove a caregiver at any time.
  • Support emails: up to 2 years after the matter is closed.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • see the information we hold about you and get a copy of it,
  • correct it (most information you can edit directly in the app),
  • have it deleted,
  • restrict or object to its use,
  • receive it in a machine-readable format (data portability): in the app, use More → Reminders & settings → Get a copy of my information,
  • withdraw your consent at any time (More → Reminders & settings → Withdraw my consent).

You can delete your account yourself in the app. To use your other rights, or if you can't open the app, email support@hoidio.com from the email address of your account. We answer within one month.

You can also complain to the Finnish data protection authority, the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi), or to the authority in your own EU country.

8. Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections. In our database, access rules ensure that your record can be read only by you and the caregivers you invited, and changed only by you. You sign in with a one-time code sent to your email, or with your Google or Apple account, so Hoidio never stores a password.

9. Children

Hoidio is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Not medical advice

Hoidio helps you organize your health information. It does not give medical advice and does not replace your doctor or pharmacist.

11. This website

hoidio.com has no cookies, analytics or advertising, and its fonts are served from the site itself. GitHub Pages, which hosts the site, handles visitors' IP addresses in its server logs to deliver the pages and keep them secure. If you email us, we use your message to answer you.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If the change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
