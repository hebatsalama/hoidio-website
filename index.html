Questions

Good to know

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Hoidio works without an account, and then everything stays on your phone.

Who is Hoidio for?

Hoidio is a medication reminder app for seniors who take several medicines, and for their adult children and other family caregivers. It keeps your medication list, dose times and refills in one place on your iPhone or Android phone, and reminds you when it’s time to take a medicine.

Where is my information kept?

In the EU, in Frankfurt, Germany. Hoidio has no advertising and no tracking, and your information is never sold.

What if I forget to take a dose?

The reminder can repeat until you mark the dose taken. If you've invited family and they have Hoidio Family, they can get an alert when a reminder isn't confirmed.

Can my family see everything?

Only what you choose. You decide how much a caregiver can see, and they join with a one-time code you give them.

Is Hoidio free?

Yes, for the person taking the medicines, always, with as many medicines as you need. Inviting caregivers is free too. Caregivers need Hoidio Family to follow you, and their first 14 days are free.

What does Hoidio Family cost, and how do I cancel?

€4.99 a month or €49.99 a year, VAT included, and the first 14 days are free. One plan covers up to two people and three caregivers. You buy it in the app, and cancel it in your Apple or Google account settings at least 24 hours before the period ends.

When can I download it?

Hoidio is coming soon for iPhone and Android, in Finnish, Swedish, English and Arabic.
