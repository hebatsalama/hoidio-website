What Hoidio does

Daily schedule and reminders

See today's doses at a glance, with reminders that can repeat so a dose isn't forgotten.

Refills and doctor visits

Get a reminder before a prescription runs out, and keep the reason and notes for each appointment.

Side effects and allergies

Log symptoms when they happen and show your doctor how often they occurred.

Measurements

Keep blood pressure, blood sugar, weight and pain readings together with your medicines.

Caregivers in the loop

Invite family members with a code. If a dose is missed, they can be alerted.

Daily “I'm OK” check

Optional: tap once a day so your family knows you're fine.

Made with care

Hoidio is designed by a licensed pharmacist in Finland. It works in English, Finnish, Swedish and Arabic. Your data is stored in the EU, there are no ads, and your information is never sold.

Hoidio helps you organize your health information. It does not replace advice from your doctor or pharmacist.
