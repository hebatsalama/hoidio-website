Up and running in three steps

  1. Add your medicines

    Type in each medicine with its dose and the times you take it.

  2. Get reminded, tick it off

    A reminder comes at each dose time and can repeat until you mark it taken.

  3. Invite your family

    Give a caregiver a one-time code. They follow along from their own phone with a caregiver subscription. The first month is free.

Made by a pharmacist, kept private

Hoidio was designed by a licensed pharmacist in Finland, for people who take several medicines a day and the families who help them.

  • Stored in the EU. Your information is kept in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • No ads, never sold. Hoidio has no advertising and no tracking.
  • Works without an account. Then everything stays on your phone.
  • Four languages. Finnish, Swedish, English and Arabic.
