Questions

Good to know

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Hoidio works without an account, and then everything stays on your phone.

Where is my information kept?

In the EU, in Frankfurt, Germany. Hoidio has no advertising and no tracking, and your information is never sold.

What if I forget to take a dose?

The reminder can repeat until you mark the dose taken. If you've invited family, they can get an alert when a reminder isn't confirmed.

Can my family see everything?

Only what you choose. You decide how much a caregiver can see, and they join with a one-time code you give them.

When can I download it?

Hoidio is coming soon for iPhone and Android, in Finnish, Swedish, English and Arabic.
