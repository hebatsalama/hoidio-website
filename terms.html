1. Who we are

Hoidio is provided by Laryngology Courses Oy (Business ID 3421195-7), Espoo, Finland. You can reach us at support@hoidio.com.

2. Who can use Hoidio

Hoidio is for people aged 18 or over. Hoidio is free for the person whose medicines it keeps track of. Everything for your own medicines is free, and so is inviting caregivers. Following someone else as a caregiver needs a caregiver subscription (section 6).

3. Hoidio is not medical advice

Hoidio helps you keep track of your medicines, doses, measurements and health notes, and reminds you when it is time to take a medicine. It does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice, and it does not replace your doctor, pharmacist or nurse.

  • Always follow the instructions from your doctor or pharmacist and on the medicine's package leaflet.
  • You are responsible for entering your medicines, doses and times correctly. Check them when anything changes.
  • A reminder can fail to arrive, for example if your phone is off, notifications are turned off, or the battery is flat. Do not rely on Hoidio alone for a medicine where a missed or extra dose could be dangerous.
  • In an emergency, call 112.

4. Your account

You can use Hoidio without an account. If you create one, you sign in with a one-time code sent to your email, or with another sign-in option shown in the app. Keep access to your email and phone safe, because anyone who can read your email can sign in. Tell us at support@hoidio.com if you think someone else has used your account.

5. Caregivers

You can invite people you trust to see your information as caregivers, and choose how much they see. You decide whom to invite and can remove a caregiver at any time. Caregiver alerts are a help, not a guarantee: an alert can be delayed or not arrive, and a caregiver may not see it in time. Hoidio does not replace care arrangements you have agreed with your family or care providers.

If you are a caregiver, use what you see only to help the person who invited you, and keep it confidential.

6. Caregiver subscription

To follow a person in Hoidio (see their medicine schedule, get alerts and summaries), a caregiver needs a subscription. The person who invites the caregiver never pays.

  • You buy the subscription in the app through Apple's App Store or Google Play, which handle the payment. The price and the subscription period are shown before you buy.
  • A new subscription starts with a free trial of one month. If you don't cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the paid subscription begins automatically.
  • One subscription lets you follow up to three people.
  • The subscription renews automatically at the end of each period unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the period ends. Cancel it in your Apple or Google account settings. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
  • If you cancel, you keep access until the end of the period you have paid for.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their own rules. Your rights as a consumer are not affected.
  • If we change the price, we tell you in advance, and you can cancel before the new price applies.
  • If the person you follow removes you as a caregiver or deletes their account, your subscription continues until you cancel it, so you can follow someone else.
